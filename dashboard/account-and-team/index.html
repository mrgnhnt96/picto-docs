Account & Team

Sign up, set up organizations and client apps, manage API keys, invite collaborators, and delete what you no longer need.

Your dashboard account owns organizations, each organization holds client apps, and each client app has the API key your app records with.

An organization groups client apps and the people who can see them. A client app is one app, or one build you want to record separately. Every session belongs to a client app.

Create an account#

Go to picto.dev/sign-up, enter Your name and Work email, and click Send my sign-in link. Open the emailed link to finish. There's no password to choose.

The account starts with no organization. The dashboard's Get started page walks you through naming your organization and first client app and choosing a plan (see Plans & Retention).

Sign-up refuses two kinds of address:

  • An address with a + in it: "Email aliases (addresses with a "+" in them) are not accepted. Sign up with your main address instead."
  • An address that reaches an inbox that already has an account: "An account already exists for this inbox. Sign in with the address you first signed up with instead." For Gmail, dots in the name and googlemail.com reach the same inbox.

Join with an invite#

An invite link looks like https://picto.dev/join?t=<code>. Open it, enter Your name and Work email, and open the sign-in link Picto emails you.

Redeeming an invite creates your account with an organization named after you, a client app called My app, and a 14-day trial that starts when you redeem it. The link is refused when it:

  • Was revoked: "That invite link is not valid. It may have been revoked — ask whoever sent it for a new one."
  • Was already used: "That invite link has already been used to create an account. Sign in instead, or ask for a new invite."
  • Is more than 14 days old: "That invite link has expired. Ask whoever sent it for a new one."

Sign in#

Go to /sign-in, enter your Email, and click Email me a sign-in link.

To use a password, click Sign in with a password instead. An account has no password until you add one: open Account in the sidebar, find Password under Sign-in, and click Add a password. If you forget it, click Forgot password? on the sign-in page and then Send reset link. A reset link works for ten minutes.

Manage organizations#

To create one, open the organization switcher in the top bar, choose New organization, enter the Organization name and click Create organization. You're its owner.

To rename one, open Organization in the sidebar, edit Name and click Save. Only the owner can.

To delete one, open Organization and click Delete organization under Danger zone, then confirm. This deletes every client app, recording and signal in it, and can't be undone. If the organization has a paid plan, Picto cancels it first so it isn't charged again. If that cancellation fails, nothing is deleted.

Manage client apps#

To create one, open the app switcher in the top bar, choose New app, enter the App name and click Create app. Only the organization's owner sees New app.

To rename one, open App settings, edit Name under App and click Save.

To delete one, open App settings and click Delete app under Danger zone, then confirm. This deletes every recording, exception and signal that belongs to it, and can't be undone.

Copy or regenerate API keys#

Your app authenticates every upload with the client app's API key. Open App settings. The API key section shows the key masked: click Reveal key to see it, or Copy key to copy it without showing it. See Quick Start for where the key goes.

To replace the key, click Regenerate key under Danger zone and confirm. Only the owner can. The old key stops working at once, and builds that still use it can't upload until you ship the new one.

Set the sampling rate and upload tokens#

App settings also holds Sampling rate, the share of app runs Picto records. See Sampling.

Account also holds Upload tokens, which picto:upload_assets uses. See Uploading Assets.

Invite collaborators#

  1. Open Organization in the sidebar.
  2. Under Collaborators, enter their email in Invite a collaborator.
  3. Click Send invite.

Picto emails them an invite. They don't need an account yet: they get access when they sign up with that address. The address is matched without regard to letter case.

Collaborators see the organization's client apps, sessions, signals and alert rules, can copy API keys, and can delete recordings. Only the owner can:

  • Invite or remove collaborators.
  • Create, rename or delete client apps, change the sampling rate, and regenerate API keys.
  • Create alert rules (see Alerts).
  • Rename, transfer or delete the organization, and manage its plan.

To remove a collaborator, click Remove collaborator next to their email and confirm.

Transfer ownership#

Under Ownership on the Organization page, click Make owner next to a collaborator, then Transfer ownership. The new owner controls billing, and the client apps and alert rules you own in the organization move to them. You stay on as a collaborator, and only the new owner can hand it back.

If there's nobody to pick, the section says No eligible collaborators. Invite someone first.

Leave an organization#

As a collaborator, open Organization and click Leave organization, then Leave. You lose access until you're invited again. An owner can't leave; transfer ownership first.

Delete your account#

  1. Open Account and click Delete account under Danger zone.
  2. Read the list of what will go. Every organization you own is deleted with everything in it, and you leave the ones you only collaborate on.
  3. Type the confirmation phrase shown in Type "…" to confirm: the name of your first owned organization, or your email if you own none.
  4. If your account has a password, enter it in Your current password and click Delete my account. If it doesn't, click Email me a confirmation link and open the link within one hour.

Any paid plan is cancelled first. If that fails, nothing is deleted. Once confirmed, deletion is immediate and nothing can be restored.
